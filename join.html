PANDA Lab / The University of Hong Kong

Join PANDA Lab

We study robotics and artificial intelligence to build reliable autonomous systems that understand and interact with the physical world. Our research focuses on manipulation and navigation, bringing together perception, learning, planning, and control.

Research Interests

Our goal is to make robots more capable, adaptable, and dependable in real-world environments. We are interested in how robots perceive their surroundings, learn transferable skills, manipulate objects, and navigate complex, changing spaces.

Explore our research demos, publications, and people to learn more about the group and how your interests connect with our work.
